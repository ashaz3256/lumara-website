Our Story

Founded by two aspiring software engineers who share a passion for both trading and computer science, Lumara represents our commitment to turning innovative insights into groundbreaking software solutions. Our journey began with a friendly competition that led to something much more meaningful.

When my associate Ismail Azfar and I decided to put our shared passion for trading and computer science to the test by racing to see who could build a trading journal website first, we discovered that collaboration was more powerful than competition. We shared ideas, solved challenges together, and pushed each other to level up our skills.

TradingJournalPro.co.uk

Our First Major Project

This project represents the culmination of our friendly competition and collaborative spirit. What started as a race between two aspiring developers became a comprehensive trading journal platform that showcases our full-stack development skills.

The result? A fully functional trading journal website that demonstrates our ability to work with modern technologies, implement secure authentication, and create an intuitive user experience for traders of all levels.

🛠 Tech Stack We Worked With

Next.js 14 React TypeScript Tailwind CSS NextAuth.js Prisma ORM PostgreSQL TradingView API Vercel bcrypt JWT

💡 Key Features We Implemented

  • Secure multi-user authentication & isolated data
  • Automated trade imports from MT4/MT5 CSV/HTML statements
  • Advanced analytics (win rates, risk metrics, performance tracking)
  • Journal system with notes, screenshots, and psychology insights
  • Responsive design across mobile and desktop
  • Real-time data visualization with TradingView integration
